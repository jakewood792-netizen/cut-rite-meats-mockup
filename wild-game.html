Alberta Wild Game Rules

We follow Alberta Government guidelines for wild game processing and need the proper tagging & paperwork included with your animal. Rules vary by species (tagging location, partner licence procedures, etc.) — if you're unsure how to tag your kill, check the current Alberta Government hunting regulations before you drop your animal off, or just ask us when you call.
