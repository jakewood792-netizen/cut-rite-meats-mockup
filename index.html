Our Story

Three generations of family butchers

Cut Rite Meats was founded in 1972 by Svend Nielsen, who trained as a butcher in Denmark before opening the shop in Calgary. His grandson, Jim "Jimmy the Butcher" Jackson, started apprenticing at age 12 and has run Cut Rite Meats for the last decade — using the same family recipes for sausage, jerky, and pepperoni.
